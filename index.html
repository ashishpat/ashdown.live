01 — About

Systematic precision, on stage and in the codebase.

With over a decade of experience as a software engineer, building everything from complex full-stack applications to iOS & Android apps, my passion for problem-solving extends beyond the screen — and a few years ago, I brought the same mindset directly into the live audio realm.

Since then, I've spent my time on the road touring across the US and internationally, mixing FOH or working as an A2/RF engineer for some of the biggest names in Nepali music.

Whether I'm mixing a full-house show, coordinating complex RF systems, tuning a venue's PA, or architecting a scalable backend, the mindset is the same: keep the routing clean, eliminate failure points under pressure, and design systems that work perfectly every night.
